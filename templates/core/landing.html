The problem

Campus issues should not disappear into informal messages.

Broken equipment, electrical faults, water problems, Wi-Fi failures, cleanliness concerns, and infrastructure damage need a reliable record. CampusFix is designed to give every issue clear ownership, visible progress, and a documented outcome.

Planned workflow

From report to resolution

A transparent seven-stage lifecycle will keep everyone informed.

01

Report

Students will submit the category, location, description, urgency, and an optional image.

02

Review & assign

Administrators will check the request, set direction, and assign appropriate maintenance staff.

03

Resolve & confirm

Staff will record progress and resolution notes; students will track the work and provide feedback.
